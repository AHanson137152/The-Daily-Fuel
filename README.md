# Daily Fuel – Nutrition Tracker Java App

Daily Fuel is a nutrition tracking application made with Java. It is designed to help users improve their eating habits and better understand what they consume each day without feeling overwhelmed. The app allows users to log meals, monitor calories, track macronutrients, compare their intake with nutrition goals, and review daily nutrition summaries through a simple, user-friendly interface.

---

## Product Vision

**FOR** anyone seeking to improve their eating habits, fitness enthusiasts, and individuals who are conscious about their health  
**WHO** needs an easy way to monitor their daily food intake and nutritional information  
**THE** Daily Fuel is a nutrition tracking application  
**THAT:**

- Makes it easy to log meals, snacks, and drinks throughout the day
- Tracks calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat
- Provides daily nutrition summaries and progress information
- Compares actual nutrition intake with personalized goals
- Helps users identify eating patterns and make healthier choices

**UNLIKE** traditional food journals or basic calorie-counting applications  
**OUR PRODUCT** provides an intuitive, user-friendly experience that combines meal tracking, nutrition analysis, personalized goals, and daily insights in one convenient application.

## What It Can Do

1. **Profile Management**
   - Create a personal nutrition profile to store health information, nutrition goals, and meal data.

2. **Add and View Meals**
   - Log meals, snacks, and drinks.
   - Record the meal name, meal type, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, date, and notes.
   - View previously logged meals.

3. **Nutrition Goals**
   - Set daily calorie, protein, carbohydrate, and fat goals.
   - Compare daily intake with saved nutrition targets.

4. **Recurring Meals**
   - Create repeating meal entries for foods consumed regularly, such as a daily breakfast or protein shake.
   - Automatically generate recurring meals according to a daily, weekly, or monthly schedule.

5. **Dashboards**
   - **Main Dashboard:** View a quick overview of daily nutrition.
   - **Meal Dashboard:** Add, view, and manage logged meals.
   - **Nutrition Goal Dashboard:** View nutrition goals and progress.
   - **Recurring Meal Dashboard:** Manage repeating meals.
   - **Nutrition Summary Dashboard:** View calorie and macronutrient totals.
   - **Search Dashboard:** Find specific meal entries.

6. **Search and Filter**
   - Search meals by name or notes.
   - Filter meals by meal type, date range, calories, protein, carbohydrates, or fat.

7. **Daily Nutrition Summaries**
   - View total calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat consumed during a selected day.
   - Identify when nutrition goals have been met or exceeded.

8. **Progress Review**
   - Review meal and nutrition information across multiple days.
   - Identify patterns that may support healthier eating choices.

## How to Use the App

Once you open the app, you will see buttons that take you to the different parts of Daily Fuel:

- **Search** – Look up meals and filter previous entries
- **Recurring Meals** – View or manage meals that repeat on a schedule
- **Nutrition Summary** – View a breakdown of calories and macronutrients
- **Meals** – Add, view, or manage meals, snacks, and drinks
- **Nutrition Goals** – Create and manage daily nutrition targets
- **Dashboard** – Return to the main nutrition overview

## How to Start

1. Navigate to the `releases/latest` page and download the ZIP file.
2. Extract the ZIP file to a location of your choice.
3. Run the application file located inside the extracted folder.
4. Start using Daily Fuel by selecting options from the main dashboard.
